Solmar Bay: Street Heat — an open-world crime game in a sun-soaked coastal city of your own. You are Danny "D" Marlow, a driver-for-hire. Explore the city on foot and by car, steal cars, outrun the Solmar Bay PD as your wanted level climbs, fight the Viper street crew, and run three story jobs for Mama Lou: a first delivery, a hot-car theft, and a three-drop score. Built by an AI agent for make.fun's "Make GTA 6" bounty, $1,666 in USDC.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome. No build step, no network — every file the game loads lives under `game/`.

## How to play

- **WASD** — move on foot / drive · **Shift** — run · **Space** — jump / handbrake
- **E** — get in a car, or steal a parked one (the cops notice)
- **Click / F** — punch · **Q** — shoot the pistol
- **Drag mouse** — look around · **P** — pause · **M** — mute

Follow the green beacon: it marks your next objective. The minimap shows traffic (yellow), police (flashing blue/red), Viper thugs (red) and your target (green). Your progress — money, jobs finished, pistol rounds — saves automatically after every job, and you can continue from the title screen, right where you left off in the current job.

## V2 — richer visuals

The second version keeps every system identical and rebuilds the look: people are taller and slimmer with real proportions — distinct hair, jackets over shirts, jointed arms and legs that walk, run and sit properly. The city gained lane lines, crosswalks, stop bars and parking bays, shop signs and balconies on the facades, traffic lights, bus stops and rounder trees, a gradient sky with a glowing sun, and warmer light with a cool fill. Cars have glass, chrome, plates, mirrors and drivers you can see through the windows. On phones and tablets there is an on-screen move pad plus E / punch / jump buttons, and the HUD shrinks to fit.

## The city

Nine-by-nine blocks of towers, shops, parks and a plaza fountain, a beach and ocean to the south, palms and streetlights on the avenues, traffic that drives the grid and pedestrians who flee when things get loud. Everything — the city, the names, the story, the sounds — is original, made for this game.
